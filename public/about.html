About us

Telecom field teams built for clean installs and dependable support.

Protech Inc fields reliable technicians for residential connectivity, telecom support, and partner service work. We handle fibre/copper lines, Wi-Fi optimization, TV, Homephone, wireless internet CPE, smart home security, SIM activations, and network support tasks.

Our focus is simple: arrive prepared, complete the work neatly, validate the service, and send back notes that help partners keep customers connected.

Snapshot

Built to support TELUS and regional telco partners with tidy installs, network-aware troubleshooting, clear documentation, and first-visit completion.

Work type: residential telecom and network support

Standard: clean handoff with proof

Goal: fewer repeat visits

What guides us

Field standards that protect the customer experience.

Every visit is handled with preparation, safety, technical validation, and documentation so partners are not left guessing what happened on site.

Principle

Clarity first

Appointment windows honored, arrival updates shared, access notes checked, and clean work areas documented with photo proof.

Principle

Safety and quality

Proper fibre/copper runs, secure mounts, light-level checks, speed tests, and device pairing before we leave.

Principle

Measure what matters

Hands-on telecom and network support experience.

  • Site-ready prep
